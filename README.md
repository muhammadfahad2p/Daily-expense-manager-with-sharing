💰 Expense Manager Pro
Expense Manager Pro is a simple and easy-to-use web app that helps you track expenses, split costs, and settle payments without confusion.
It is made for people who want to know where their money goes and who owes whom, without complicated setups or accounts.
You can use it directly in your browser on mobile or desktop.


🌟 What can this app do?
🧾 Record Expenses
Add daily expenses with date, amount, and description
Select who paid and who consumed
Organize expenses by groups (family, friends, office, trip, etc.)


🤝 Split & Settle Payments
Automatically calculates who needs to pay whom
Mark payments as Paid
Once everything is paid, archive the settlement safely
Keep settlement history for future reference


📊 Clear Summaries
See expenses by day, month, or year
View total spending and balances clearly
Easy-to-understand settlement summary


🗂 Settled History
View past settled expenses
Restore a settled batch if needed
Delete old settlements safely (no accidental loss)


☁️ Backup & Restore
Optional Google Drive backup
Restore all data anytime
Archived (settled) data is backed up separately for safety


📤 Export Your Data
Export expenses and summaries to Excel
Share your expense file easily


📱 Mobile Friendly
Designed especially for mobile phones
No unwanted keyboard pop-ups
Large buttons and clean layout
Works smoothly on touch screens


🔒 Safe & Private
No signup required
No server or account needed
Your data stays in your browser
You control backups and deletions


👨‍👩‍👧 Who is this app for?
Individuals tracking personal expenses
Families managing shared costs
Roommates splitting rent and bills
Friends on trips
Anyone who wants simple expense tracking without complexity
